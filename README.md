# Not Enough Rain Sounds · 更好的雨声

A Minecraft sound mod. In developing.

## Testing with audio resource packs

The mod loads its custom audio from enabled resource packs. For Minecraft 1.21.1, copy the ZIP files you want from [soundResource/resourcepacks](soundResource/resourcepacks) into your instance's resource pack folder. Enable the Ground background pack and the Wood C Soft-Tone pack in **Options → Resource Packs**. Compare Metal5 Clear-Hits and Metal5 Reference-Mix with one metal pack enabled at a time. If a pack provides no replacement for a weather sound, Minecraft plays its original sound. Material rain and plant drips require their corresponding pack events.

The [legacy audio archive](soundResource/archives/NERS-Legacy-Audio) holds the previous assets and attribution. It stays outside the mod and development instances.
